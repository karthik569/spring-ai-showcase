# Enterprise AI & Remote Work Policies 2026

## 1. Remote Work & Equipment
Employees are entitled to a $1,500 annual home office equipment stipend. Stipends cover ergonomic chairs, dual-monitor setups, noise-cancelling headphones, and standing desk converters.
Reimbursements must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of purchase.

## 2. Learning & Conference Budget
Every full-time team member receives a $2,500 annual budget for attending technical conferences, purchasing books, taking certifications (e.g. AWS, GCP, Spring certifications), and taking online training courses.

## 3. Leave Policy & Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Standard PTO: 25 days of annual leave plus 10 company holidays.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary and secondary caregivers.
- Mental Wellness Days: 4 designated company-wide wellness days per calendar year.

## 4. AI Tool Usage Guidelines
- Employees may use approved AI coding assistants (such as Antigravity and Spring AI) for company codebase development.
- Sensitive customer PII (Personally Identifiable Information), raw credentials, and production secret keys must never be pasted into unapproved third-party LLM web interfaces.
